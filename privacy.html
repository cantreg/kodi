Privacy policy

Home media centre · last updated 25 October 2026

Who uses it

The app is a personal media centre (Kodi on a Raspberry Pi) in one household. Only the owner signs in with it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent it reads the owner's YouTube account data through the YouTube Data API: subscriptions, playlists, liked videos and public channel and video information.

How the data is used

The data is only shown on the owner's TV, to browse and play videos. It is not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens and cached lists are stored only on the device itself.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out in the YouTube add-on deletes the tokens from the device.
